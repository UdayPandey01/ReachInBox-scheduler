ReachInbox Scheduler

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

ReachInbox Scheduler is a demo project built for the ReachInbox / Outbox Labs internship assignment. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

Information we collect

  • Google account: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to create your account and show who is logged in.
  • Emails you schedule: the sender, recipients, subject, body and any attachments you add, plus their status and send times.
  • Slack (optional): if you connect Slack, we store your workspace name, the channel you choose and an access token, used only to post rate-limit alerts to that channel.

How emails are sent

All emails are sent through Ethereal, a fake SMTP testing service. Messages are captured for preview and are never delivered to real inboxes.

How we use and store data

  • Data is used only to run the app's features: scheduling, sending, search and Slack alerts.
  • It is stored in a PostgreSQL database, a Redis queue and an Elasticsearch search index on servers used for this project.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We only request basic Google scopes (email and profile). We cannot read your Gmail or Google Drive.

Cookies

We use a single secure, http-only session cookie to keep you signed in, and short-lived cookies to protect the Google and Slack sign-in flows. There are no tracking or analytics cookies.

Your choices

  • You can disconnect Slack at any time from the dashboard.
  • You can revoke the app's Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To delete your account and data, email us and we will remove it.
